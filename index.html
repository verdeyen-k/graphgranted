How to play

  • The guy walks to the right and never stops. Get him to the 🏁 flag.
  • You can't touch him. Grab the graph instead: ✋ Move slides the line and 🔄 Rotate turns it. Then press ▶ Go.
  • The yellow dot runs along the line from one side to the other. How high it is makes the number.
  • Red slides left when the number is high, and right when it's low.
  • Blue slides left when the number is low, and right when it's high.
  • Orange ignores the graph, but it falls and gets pushed around.
  • Grey never moves.
  • While you plan, faint outlines show where red and blue will be when the dot gets to the end.
